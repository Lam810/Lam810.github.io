Zeteng Lin

Ph.D. Student, Data Science and Analytics

Information Hub, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

I am a Ph.D. student in Data Science and Analytics at HKUST(GZ), supervised by Prof. Jing Tang. My thesis committee chair is Prof. Danyang Xie, and I am also co-supervised by Dr. Yujun Cai at Meta.

My research interests include artificial intelligence, computational social science, digital economy, and AI evaluation. I am especially interested in how large AI models behave in social, organizational, and economic decision contexts, and how to evaluate these behaviors with empirical and computational methods.
