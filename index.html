I am a Ph.D. candidate in Data Science and Analytics at HKUST(GZ), supervised by Prof. Jing Tang and Yujun Cai. My Ph.D. committee members include Prof. Danyang Xie and Prof. Tang Nan.

My research focuses on trustworthy multimodal AI, particularly on evaluating what foundation models truly perceive, infer, and simulate across images and videos.
