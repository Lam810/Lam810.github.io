Zeteng Lin

Ph.D. Student, Data Science and Analytics

Information Hub, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

I am a Ph.D. student in Data Science and Analytics at HKUST(GZ), supervised by Prof. Jing Tang. My thesis committee chair is Prof. Danyang Xie, and I am also co-supervised by Dr. Yujun Cai at Meta.

My research interests include artificial intelligence, computational social science, digital economy, and AI evaluation.

Research Interests

  • Large language models, AI evaluation, and model behavior analysis
  • Computational social science, survey simulation, and cultural response distributions
  • Multimodal reasoning, visual-document RAG, and document intelligence
