Zeteng Lin

Ph.D. Student, Data Science and Analytics

Information Hub, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

I am a Ph.D. student in Data Science and Analytics at HKUST(GZ), supervised by Prof. Jing Tang. My Ph.D. committee chair is Prof. Danyang Xie, and I am also co-supervised by Dr. Yujun Cai at Meta.

I am the Editorial Board Member at Journal of Global ESG Innovation (ISSN-3135-2995-Print).

Furthermore, I was a Visiting Graduate Student (VER Student) of SUSTech under Professor Fengliang Li.

I received my M.Phil. degree supervised by Prof. Jing Tang and Prof. Yangqiu Song.

I was an Assistant Research Fellow at SUSTech under Prof. Chen Jie, the Associate Dean at Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. Our research has been covered by the South China Morning Post (南华早报).

I was also a Policy Research Assistant at the Institute for Economic and Social Research (IESR), Jinan University.
