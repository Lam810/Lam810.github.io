Zeteng Lin

Ph.D. Student, Data Science and Analytics

Information Hub, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

I am a Ph.D. student in Data Science and Analytics at HKUST(GZ), supervised by Prof. Jing Tang and Meta Scientist Yujun Cai . My Ph.D. committee chair is Prof. Danyang Xie and Prof. Tang Nan.

My research lies at the intersection of trustworthy multimodal AI and computational social science, with a particular focus on evaluating what foundation models truly perceive, infer, and simulate.
