About

Researching AI as both a technical system and a social force.

I am a Ph.D. student in Data Science and Analytics at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), supervised by Prof. Jing Tang. My thesis committee chair is Prof. Danyang Xie, and I am also co-supervised by Dr. Yujun Cai at Meta.

I serve as an Editorial Board Member of the Journal of Global ESG Innovation. Before my current Ph.D. study, I was a visiting graduate student at the Southern University of Science and Technology under Prof. Fengliang Li. My earlier M.Phil. study was supervised by Prof. Jing Tang and Prof. Yangqiu Song. I also worked as an Assistant Research Fellow at SUSTech under Prof. Jie Chen.

My work combines empirical research, computational modeling, and system building. I am especially interested in AI governance, economics of AI, digital platforms, large language models, and evaluation methods for model behavior.

Research Focus

Current directions

AI and digital economy

Studying how AI capabilities, platforms, and automation reshape firms, markets, productivity, and public policy.

Computational social science

Using large-scale data and computational methods to model social behavior, survey responses, cultural variation, and institutional outcomes.

LLM and multimodal evaluation

Designing tests and systems that reveal how models reason over language, images, documents, options, and decision contexts.

Technical Stack

Methods and systems I use

01

LLM evaluation pipelines

Benchmark construction, prompt-controlled experiments, option-level robustness checks, response distribution analysis, and automated judging workflows.

02

Survey simulation and social data

Cultural response distribution modeling, questionnaire design, post-stratification thinking, and validation against empirical survey signals.

03

Multimodal and document AI

Visual-document RAG, layout-aware retrieval, image-question reasoning, long-context evidence extraction, and evaluation on visually rich materials.

04

Causal and empirical analysis

Panel-style reasoning, contextual causal evidence, policy analysis, ESG and digital-economy measurement, and reproducible statistical workflows.
