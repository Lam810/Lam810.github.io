Zeteng Lin

Ph.D. Candidate, Data Science and Analytics

Information Hub, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou)

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Data Science and Analytics at HKUST(GZ), supervised by Prof. Jing Tang and Meta Scientist Yujun Cai. My Ph.D. committee chair is Prof. Danyang Xie and Prof. Tang Nan.

My research focuses on trustworthy multimodal AI, particularly on evaluating what foundation models truly perceive, infer, and simulate across images and videos.

Open Source

Desensitized engineering prototypes and research systems. Projects marked Ascend NPU were verified on Ascend 910C hardware, not merely documented as compatible.

  • factor-rag

    Structure-aware Markdown chunking that keeps tables, headers and figure captions intact, with a reproducible benchmark scoring any chunking strategy on structural fidelity.

    RAG Document AI Python
  • Silicon Respondents on CFPS

    Fine-tunes language models against real CFPS response distributions so generated survey answers match a population's option proportions, not just its modal answer.

    LoRA Computational Social Science Qwen
  • AgenticVideoRAG

    An agentic framework for long-video question answering that plans missing evidence, retrieves targeted clips, and verifies grounding before answering.

    Video QA Agents RAG
  • NexusFlow

    A visual workflow studio for assistant pipelines — intent routing, retrieval, tool calls and model orchestration — running on self-hosted vLLM on Ascend 910C.

    Ascend NPU Workflow TypeScript On-device AI
  • visual-document-rag

    RAG for visually rich documents where layout, formulas and tables survive the pipeline, ported from CUDA to Ascend NPUs and tested on 910C.

    Ascend NPU RAG Document AI CANN
  • Cantonese ASR Collaboration

    A reproducible Cantonese speech-recognition system fine-tuned and distilled from Whisper-small, from data preparation through offline packaging.

    Speech Whisper Low-resource
  • SoloChart Collaboration

    A single-file page that turns a plain-language question into an interactive chart, backed by self-hosted vLLM on Ascend 910C and carrying no API credential.

    Ascend NPU Analytics vLLM Zero-build
